The concept

Every participant is matched with one student from another university eight weeks before RESTO. They complete three small tasks together — and stay anonymous until the Welcome Event in Helsinki.

The mechanic

Your connection arrives at a quarter of their resolution. Every completed clue loads a little more of them. The last quarter only resolves in person.

Why it matters

The technology is not the experience. It only creates the anticipation — so the first real conversation in Helsinki starts somewhere other than “where are you from”.
