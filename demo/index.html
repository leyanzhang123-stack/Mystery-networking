The idea

Everyone gets two connections eight weeks before RESTO: the person they picked, and the person who picked them. Three small tasks, weeks of chat, and no idea who they are talking to.

The rule

No names, no schools, no photos, no Instagram. The whole thing collapses the moment someone gives it away.

Why bother

By the time they land in Helsinki they have spent two months talking to someone they have never seen. That first handshake starts a long way past “so, where are you from”.
