Layer 1 · Team Online Cafés

Sixteen teams. One wheel. Two new cafés.

Every team of four, from a different university, sends its captain to spin the wheel. Wherever it lands is the team you will meet first. Then a Student Guide opens the chat, drops in a topic, and lets the conversation do the rest.

Where it sits

Three moments in eight weeks.

The wheel turns in week 2. The two cafés follow in weeks 4 and 6, between the Mystery Tasks.

Week 2 · Café matching

Captain, spin for your team.

Each team spins once. Whoever you land on becomes your Café 1 partner in week 4. The team that lands on you becomes your Café 2 partner in week 6. So every team ends up with exactly two.

Nobody can be chosen twice, and two teams can never pick each other. The wheel only lands on a fair choice, so nobody is left without a partner at the end.

All sixteen teams are matched.Everyone has a Café 1 partner and a Café 2 partner. Look at the ring: it closes into loops, so no team is left out.

Each arrow goes from the team that spun to the team it landed on. Follow an arrow out for Café 1, follow one in for Café 2.

Weeks 4 and 6

Then the Guide opens the chat.

Once the wheel has matched the teams, a Student Guide adds both teams to a WhatsApp group and gives them a topic. The goal is simple: break the ice and start networking.
